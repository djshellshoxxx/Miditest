# MIDItest Wiki

MIDItest (Circuit Drift Labs) is a local-first browser MIDI monitor, controller diagnostic bench and troubleshooting workspace.
It reports what connected MIDI hardware sends: what arrives, how consistently, on which channel and rate, and whether the
measured behaviour changes over time. It reports observations, not a repair diagnosis (README).
